About Us

Olive & Ember started as two sisters cooking Sunday lunch for the neighbours. People kept asking for seconds, so we opened a small kitchen of our own.

Everything on the menu is made from scratch each morning, with produce from local farmers whenever we can. Come hungry, stay as long as you like.
